## The problem

Electronic case reporting implementation requires client onboarding, technical readiness, data-quality follow-up, external approval steps, and go-live planning to move together. Each deployment involves a different client context while shared dependencies must remain visible across the portfolio.

## My responsibilities

I led electronic case reporting implementation programs across approximately 50 healthcare clients, coordinating work from kickoff and onboarding through soft and hard go-live. I connected client-facing and technical stakeholders, tracked readiness, and managed follow-up needed for reporting workflows and implementation requirements.

## Execution approach

I led milestones across concurrent client implementations, maintained visibility into dependencies and readiness activities, and drove follow-up for data-quality and external approval needs. The work required clear communication across stakeholders and practical sequencing of technical and client delivery steps.

## Outcome and lessons learned

This work advanced repeatable client progress toward go-live across approximately 50 healthcare clients while keeping program dependencies and reporting requirements in view. It reinforced that healthcare implementation delivery depends on a shared, explicit definition of readiness—not simply completing an isolated technical task.
